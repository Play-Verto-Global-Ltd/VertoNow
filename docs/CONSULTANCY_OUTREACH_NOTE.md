## For the intro email

> Strat-Analytics builds its recommendations on 100–150 primary interviews per
> engagement, across the value chain, in multiple geographies — and does that
> work itself rather than farming it out. That's the differentiator, and it's
> also the bottleneck: a forty-five minute ask converts only a fraction of an
> outreach list, the small distributors and installers who hold the channel
> economics are the least likely to take a call, and every market outside the
> team's own languages costs more to reach than it returns.
>
> Play Verto turns part of that interview programme into a three-minute
> experience people finish on their phone. It's built from the firm's own
> discussion guide — questions kept word for word, only the answer type chosen
> for them — it runs under the firm's brand, and the answers come back
> structured: counted, segmented by country and channel, open ends already
> themed, exported to CSV or Google Sheets. The depth interviews don't change.
> What changes is how much of the outreach list converts, and how much of the
> value chain is reachable at all.
>
> It isn't a prototype. Play Verto has delivered 30+ global enterprise projects
> for clients including LEGO, Nike, Unilever, UNICEF, UNESCO and Deloitte, and
> around 214,000 individual responses sit in the platform today — including
> 126,895 sessions in five languages for the UAE Ministry of Education, and
> 28,574 respondents across seven countries for Wall's. Completion runs around
> 85%, against the 20–30% a conventional survey gets.
>
> The attached page has a live Verto embedded in it — worth ninety seconds,
> because the experience is the argument. If it lands, the next step is a short
> call with whoever runs their primary research: we'd walk through a live or
> recent engagement, find where interview volume and fatigue bite hardest, and
> scope a pilot on one engagement from there.

## Notes for Michael, not for the email

- **The pitch is deliberately not "outsource your interviews."** Both firms do
  their primary research in-house on purpose — Strat-Analytics says the work is
  "carried out exclusively by the firm", MattersGraph that "consultants are also
  in-house researchers". Positioning Verto as a replacement argues against their
  own differentiator. It's a second instrument for the part of the list a depth
  interview was never going to reach.
- **Verto doesn't recruit.** No panel, no sample sourcing, no quotas, no
  incentive handling. The page says so plainly, because a research buyer will
  ask inside the first ten minutes and finding out later is worse. They bring
  the names; we convert more of them.
- **White-labelling is in; the co-funded build and revenue share are not.** That
  is a commercial negotiation to have with Amit, not something to put in a
  document going cold to a founder who asked what value we'd bring.
- **The "audio narration" line from the NASVI paragraph is deliberately absent.**
  The platform has no text-to-speech or audio playback anywhere. Whatever NASVI
  had, we can't demonstrate it today, and a founder who probes it would find
  that out. NASVI is on the page as a reach story — two languages, mobile-first,
  low literacy, live dashboard — all of which stands up.
